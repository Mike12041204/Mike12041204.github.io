Projects


GPU-Accelerated Clique Mining | cuQC

The first GPU-accelerated maximal quasi-clique mining algorithm. This program can accelerates enumeration by up to 4,992x compared to the CPU algorithm. Developed by the High-Performance Data Mining Lab at Rowan University and external researchers.
